The Astro PP must ensure that core Astro meets the needs of USAFA and the Space Force, and ensure that the entirety of the Astro curriculum maintains the highest standards of academic preparation for majors and accreditation.

Permanent Professors also fulfill USAFA's mandate of commissioning cadets with character, aptitude, and intellectual rigor; both directly, by serving in cadet-facing roles that keep the institution running smoothly, and indirectly as Department leaders.
